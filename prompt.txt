### Email Body Structure — `likely-fit` and `explore-with-caveats`

**1. Opening (personal, from Dave — not from a system):**
> "[firstName] — I reviewed your results and wanted to make sure you have the
> full picture before any conversations happen."

**2. Pathway result** — 2–3 sentences from `content.body` for their pathway

**3. Equity illustration section**
Heading: "What this could look like"

Show all three age bands as a formatted list or simple table.
Flag their specific band with a note:
> "(based on the age range you indicated)"

Include `illustrationDisclaimer` below the bands.

**4. Three things to know before any conversation**
Render `content.anticipationBullets` as a numbered list.

**5. What most families don't know**
Render `content.whatFamiliesDontKnow` as a blockquote or indented paragraph.

**6. Next step** by `ctaType`:
- `specialist`:
  > "I'll reach out personally within one business day to discuss whether an
  > introduction to a Colorado specialist makes sense for your situation.
  > If you'd prefer to reach me directly:
  > Dave@ElderLifeTransitions.net | 720-258-6001"
- `elder-law`:
  > Same structure but references elder law attorney introduction.
- `elt-direct`:
  > Same structure but references direct conversation with Dave.

**7. Compliance footer:**
Full `FUNDING_RM_DISCLAIMER` text in small/muted styling.

ELT contact block:
`Dave@ElderLifeTransitions.net | elderlifetransitions.net | 720-258-6001`

### Email Body Structure — `medicaid-flag`

- Omit equity illustration entirely
- Lead with the Medicaid interaction explanation (warm but direct)
- Emphasize: elder law attorney first, ELT as coordinator
- This family needs to slow down before proceeding — the tone should reflect that
- Include full compliance footer

---

### Email Body Structure — `not-a-fit`

- Omit equity illustration entirely
- Lead with validation — this is not a dead end
- Introduce VA Aid and Attendance as the most likely alternative:
  > "Veterans and surviving spouses may qualify for $1,200 to $2,200 per month
  > through the VA Aid and Attendance benefit — regardless of home ownership.
  > Dave can help determine whether this applies."
- Offer Dave's direct conversation
- Include full compliance footer
